## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a deeply grounded, trustworthy, and tactile digital procurement environment tailored for agricultural producers, commission agents (arhtiyas), and rural grain merchants. Rather than relying on generic modern tech aesthetics, cold glassmorphism, or synthetic neon gradients, the interface draws direct inspiration from the honest physical realities of Indian agricultural markets: sun-drenched grain yards, unbleached khadi ledger paper, hand-stamped weighbridge receipts, and rich crop foliage.

The style is **Tactile / High-Contrast Grounded**. It embraces physical paper surfaces, structured organic borders, generous tap targets, and earthy pigment accents. The emotional resonance is one of unyielding reliability, commercial clarity, and agrarian dignity, ensuring farmers and traders feel absolute certainty during high-value trade confirmations, weighbridge logging, and direct account settlements under harsh outdoor daylight.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from raw agricultural matter—lush standing crops, warm baked terracotta tiles, dried grain husks, and unbleached parchment ledger sheets.

- **Primary (`#15803D`)**: Lush field green representing crop vitality, verified quality grading, trade settlement, and fiscal security.
- **Secondary (`#B45309`)**: Warm earth terracotta evoking kiln-fired clay, mandi brick paths, and key transactional emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#C2410C`)**: Deep sun-baked terracotta for urgent alerts, live bidding counters, and critical inspection notices.
- **Neutral (`#78716C`)**: Warm stone earthy gray, grounding muted typography, field borders, and inactive states.

### Named Cultural Palette
- **Parchment Base (`#FBF7EE`)**: The unbleached organic paper background that prevents screen glare in direct sunlight.
- **Parchment Surface (`#F8F4EB`)**: Tactile card and container surface tier.
- **Parchment Border (`#E7DFCF`)**: Crisp physical stroke defining paper edges, reminiscent of printed mandi receipts.
- **Charcoal Ink (`#1F2937`)**: Deep slate for maximum contrast typography, ensuring effortless readability for fatigued eyes under open skies.
- **Grain Amber (`#D97706`)**: Saturated harvest gold for pending status, moisture level highlights, and active bidding chips.

## Typography

Typography balances robust readability with high-density data parsing.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines & Labels)**: Provides open counters, tall x-height, and decisive geometric strokes. Under high glare conditions, characters maintain distinct structural boundaries, preventing misreading of lot numbers, rates, and crop varieties.
- **Manrope (Body & Data Numerals)**: Offers clean, uniform tabular digits for currency amounts (₹/quintal), moisture percentages, weighbridge net weights, and bag tallies. Its humanist tone ensures extended legibility across long commodity ledgers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an assertive 8px spacing rhythm engineered specifically for rugged outdoor use, one-handed smartphone operation, and physical touch accuracy.

- **Grid Architecture**: 4-column fluid layout on mobile (360px–639px), adapting to an 8-column layout on mandi inspection tablets (640px–1023px), and a centered 12-column grid capped at 1160px on trade desks.
- **Outdoor Touch Targets**: All primary transactional buttons, audio prompts, and lot selectors maintain a minimum height of `4rem` (64px) to accommodate dusty fingers, field handling, and screen protectors.
- **Safe Margins**: Mobile screens maintain a strict `1.25rem` outer margin to prevent edge tapping errors, with sticky bottom transaction bars elevated cleanly above navigation areas.

## Elevation & Depth

To remain legible in bright sunlit environments, this design system avoids diffuse, low-contrast drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **physical borders, warm tonal layering, and directional paper-cast shadows**.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Parchment base `#FBF7EE` without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Commodity Cards & Receipt Containers)**: Parchment surface `#F8F4EB` framed by a physical `1px solid #E7DFCF` border. Shadow uses an earth-tinted tactile offset: `0 2px 4px rgba(120, 113, 108, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Bids & Floating Action Panels)**: Raised white container `#FFFFFF` with a `1.5px solid #E7DFCF` border and a crisp tactile drop shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.12), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Weighbridge Gate Slips)**: Grounded overlay framed by a `2px solid #B45309` accent boundary with a deep earthy silhouette shadow: `0 12px 28px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements **Roundedness 2 (Rounded)**, balancing physical paper ledger familiarity with digital ergonomics:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Applied to standard interactive chips, form fields, and quantitative counters.
- **Container Radius (`1rem`)**: Applied to commodity lot cards, inspection certificates, and trade confirmation panels.
- **Sheet Radius (`1.5rem`)**: Applied to bottom payment drawers, weighbridge summary sheets, and modal viewports.
- **Pill Geometry (`full`)**: Reserved exclusively for status indicators (e.g., "Quality Passed", "Bid Live", "Payment Dispatched") and voice-search triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Transactional Button**: Background `#15803D`, text `#FFFFFF`, minimum height `4rem` (64px), border-radius `0.75rem` with a subtle tactile border (`1px solid #166534`). Active state: `#166534` with an inset press offset.
- **Secondary Procurement Button**: Background `#B45309`, text `#FFFFFF`, height `3.5rem` (56px) for secondary trading actions like Counter-Offer or Sample Request.
- **Parchment Ghost Button**: Background `#F8F4EB`, text `#1F2937`, border `1.5px solid #E7DFCF`.

### Cards & Procurement Slips
- Structured on `#F8F4EB` with a `1px solid #E7DFCF` border.
- Features a header block carrying high-visibility crop badges, moisture ratings, bag counts, and dynamic ₹/quintal pricing in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Includes a perforated visual divider for trade slips ready to be printed or shared via messaging apps.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height set to `2.75rem` (44px) for rapid tactile filtering of commodities (Wheat, Mustard, Chana, Soybean).
- Inactive: Background `#FBF7EE`, border `1px solid #E7DFCF`, text `#1F2937`.
- Active: Background `#15803D`, border `1px solid #166534`, text `#FFFFFF` with bold checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Quantity Steppers
- Height locked to `3.75rem` (60px) with `#1F2937` typography at `18px`.
- Crisp `1.5px solid #E7DFCF` border, transitioning to a focused state of `2px solid #15803D` without glowing artificial neons.
- Stepper buttons for weight adjustments (+/- 50kg, +/- 1 quintal) feature physical dual tactile buttons (`48px × 48px`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Sized at `28px × 28px` with a `2px solid #78716C` stroke.
- Checked state fills with `#15803D`, displaying an unbleached cream checkmark icon.

### Audio & Vernacular Assistance Module
- A persistent floating action pill (`56px` height) with `#B45309` accents, offering voice commands and dynamic audio playback of lot prices and mandi rules in regional languages.
